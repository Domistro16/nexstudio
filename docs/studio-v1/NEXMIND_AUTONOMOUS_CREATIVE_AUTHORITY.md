## 6. Studio final quality / taste

There is **no aggregate quality score**. Autonomous acceptance requires all independent evidence planes to pass: hard gates; craft; taste; novelty/template divergence; confidence; complete hash-bound video/audio evidence; and Studio-specific machine↔human calibration.

Minimum autonomous quality floors are craft mean ≥9.0, taste mean ≥9.0, every scored craft/taste dimension ≥8.5, critical dimensions ≥9.0, novelty ≥6.5, template similarity ≤4.0, zero LOW-confidence scored dimensions and COMPLETE multimodal evidence.

## 7. Studio-specific calibration

Twelve real blind reviews only make correlation eligible. Routine no-human lock requires at least 36 non-synthetic reviews, 12 distinct productions, 6 reviews per launch family, mean correlation ≥0.80, per-dimension correlation ≥0.60, mean absolute error ≤0.70, max per-dimension error ≤1.00, max machine optimism bias ≤0.35 and **zero machine false accepts**. Synthetic reviews never count.

Current real calibration records: **0**. Therefore autonomous paid-production taste is not certified.

## 8. Autonomous repair

Quality failures return a structured repair request with implicated slots, issues, revision plan, quality reasons and prior state/board hashes. Studio re-enters P8 with the repair context. Two creative repair rounds are allowed; a third failed round becomes `PRODUCTION_FAILED`. Quality thresholds never decrease. Human review cannot rescue machine-detected weak work.

The certified implementation uses safe full P8 re-entry with repair context. Selective minimal department replay remains an optimization not yet separately certified.

## 9. Human escalation

Three cases are deliberately distinct:

1. **Quality failure:** autonomous repair or fail closed; human override forbidden.
2. **Calibration not yet proven:** blind human calibration bridge remains temporarily mandatory after automated quality passes.
3. **Calibration proven + pure exceptional taste ambiguity:** one independent blind human judgment may be requested by the Final Producer; routine jobs do not require a human.
