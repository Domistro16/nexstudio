# NexStudio — NexMind P8 canonical non-Character source

NexMind P8 is the sole top-level creative authority. Whiteboard, Explainer and Editorial Motion are execution bodies: they may bind supported executable primitives or fail/replan, but may not invent Brand, story, camera, motion, world/object semantics or commercial taste decisions.

Final judgment occurs only after encoded review media exists. Final Producer and the independent perceptual auditor receive hash-bound temporal frames, native audio and reference evidence from the exact reviewed MP4. Creative Lock binds those reviewed bytes. Customer delivery promotes the exact approved MP4 bytes; no post-lock creative rerender exists.

Model/provider identity is deployment configuration, never source architecture. Configure `NEXMIND_MODEL_REGISTRY_JSON`, provider-neutral creative/review lanes, or exact role overrides. Final Producer and Independent Perceptual Auditor are separate role/process invocations but may use the same sufficiently capable model identity. Both must explicitly support image + native-audio input and pass a fresh build-bound boot attestation. One provider/model/API key may serve every NexMind role when its declared capabilities satisfy those roles; multiple models remain optional deployment configuration. Art/TTS/music/browser/ffmpeg capabilities are production-scoped readiness requirements and are rechecked before debit.

Source Intelligence preserves the full indexed corpus. Finite model context is handled by relevance/source-balanced batching with explicit omission metadata; ingestion does not silently discard first/last-N evidence. Visual and Art receive hash-bound raw visual references when relevant.

Commercial 9.5 release is a separate evidence gate. Local regression/contract QA cannot substitute for compatible live creative/review routes, encoded sealed production films, independent perceptual judging and blind human calibration.

Character/NexStick is external to this repair and must remain byte-identical to its frozen archive.

## Autonomous creative repair governance

Initial Story/Visual/Art competition may compare genuinely different candidates. Once a strongest rejected Visual or Art candidate is selected as the repair anchor, bounded local repair is surgical: repair that exact anchor, preserve unaffected decisions, and do not reopen candidate competition merely to obtain a different answer. Resolved repair requirements remain sticky within the local lineage so later repair attempts may not silently regress them. Structural Director-output contract misses use a separate bounded contract-repair budget and do not consume a creative-quality attempt.

Story/Visual concept reviews may reject implausible physics, unsafe staging, genericity, continuity failures, weak causality, pacing defects, or other concept-stage craft failures. They may not require a concept-stage Director to fabricate empirical evidence that can only exist in production (for example measured physical response times or practical food/lab tests). The concept must state a plausible observable behavior; empirical proof is carried forward as a required downstream production validation. Mixed issues are split so the creative defect remains blocking while the empirical proof requirement is deferred rather than discarded.

Repeated Producer critiques are canonicalized before another repair call. Local exhaustion automatically climbs only to the nearest causal owner with remaining local budget and continues in the same Showrunner state, preserving accepted upstream decisions. For example, exhausted Art Direction first replans Visual Concept in place; only if the causal owners are also exhausted does escalation reach Story and use the separately bounded full-strategy restart budget (default 3 broader Story rounds). Local quality gates and per-department attempt limits are not weakened. Art support/decoration counts are authored composition/provisioning data, not hard-coded house rejection quotas; clutter and hierarchy are judged semantically.

## Performance governance

Creative quality gates remain authoritative, but wall-clock cost is now governed explicitly. A simple short-form brief retains genuine initial candidate competition without automatically expanding to three Story strategies merely because normal brief fields are populated. Longer/evidence-heavy briefs may expand the competition adaptively. Once Story, Visual or Art enters local repair, the strongest rejected candidate is repaired surgically rather than reopening broad candidate competition.

Independent Producer reviews of already-generated candidates may execute with bounded concurrency; this changes scheduling, not judgment. The review parallelism is deployment-configurable through `NEXMIND_REVIEW_PARALLELISM` and defaults to 3. Provider retries are bounded independently of creative attempt budgets; `NEXMIND_PROVIDER_MAX_RETRIES` defaults to 1 and `NEXMIND_PROVIDER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` defaults to 90 seconds. Provider transport retries never count as creative-quality attempts.

Provider JSON schemas are request-aware: a surgical Visual/Art repair contract requires exactly one candidate, while initial competition requires the exact adaptive candidate budget requested by the Director. This prevents schema-level retry churn from contradicting creative-repair governance. Every P8 result now retains provider call count, accumulated provider latency, retry/failure/schema-repair counts and per-task breakdown. The blind preflight also records wall-clock elapsed time plus full progress-event payloads so performance regressions can be diagnosed from one report.

## Final end-to-end repair and recovery law

The same competition/repair law now applies through Cinematography, Editorial Rhythm, Motion Performance and Sound Direction: initial creation may use genuine bounded candidate competition, while local repair is anchored to exactly one previous candidate. Director schemas, semantic contracts and orchestration agree on that single-candidate repair shape. Candidate-set non-diversity is a recoverable creative-set defect: it opens a materially new competition and never commits a near-duplicate set.

The executable temporal-storyboard boundary accepts exactly one executable camera binding and one executable edit binding per story beat. Multi-shot or multi-edit creative ideas must be authored across beats rather than passed downstream in a shape the execution body cannot execute.

Causal-owner escalation is issue-code/semantic based and must not infer Story ownership from structural metadata such as `beat_id`. Story restarts therefore occur only for actual Story-owned defects or when all nearer causal owners are exhausted.

Provider-backed department calls are transactional. A provider transport/schema failure restores the safe pre-call checkpoint so no incomplete candidate/review state is committed and no creative-quality attempt is consumed. The blind preflight reuses the same provider and automatically resumes bounded transient provider failures within the same command; configuration/capability failures are never misclassified as transient transport failures. Director output-contract exhaustion is likewise distinct from provider unavailability and routes through bounded structural/local repair.

Direct Python preflight binds the checked-in frozen family execution authority when an explicit workflow capability graph is absent. Explicit production-workflow authority always overrides the fallback; the fallback does not grant public-production eligibility or invent capabilities.
