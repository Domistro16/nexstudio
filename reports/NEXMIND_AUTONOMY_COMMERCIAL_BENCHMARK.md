## What this branch improved without pretending it changed the scores

- Story no longer commits the first legal narrative answer; three narrative strategies compete.
- Decision-slot ownership is enforced in P8 without modifying frozen P0.
- A Studio-specific final quality vector prevents average-score laundering.
- Calibrated autonomy requires substantially more evidence than the old 12-review correlation eligibility floor.
- Human approval cannot override machine-detected weak craft/taste.
- Bounded creative repair is wired into the paid Studio workflow.
- Pure calibrated ambiguity has an exceptional-human path; routine production does not.
- Long-term Creative Memory is promoted-only and cannot self-modify live production.

These are architecture/control improvements. They do not substitute for the missing blind commercial films.
