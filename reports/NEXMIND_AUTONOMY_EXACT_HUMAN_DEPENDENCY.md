## Today

Routine per-job blind human review remains required **only after** the film has already passed automated hard/quality gates, because Studio-specific machine↔human taste calibration currently has zero real eligible blind reviews.

A human cannot approve around a machine-detected quality defect. Those jobs must be repaired autonomously within the two-round budget or fail closed.

## Automatic removal condition

Routine human review disappears without another code redesign only when the calibration registry simultaneously satisfies the 36-review / 12-production / four-family coverage requirements and all correlation, error, optimism-bias and zero-false-accept thresholds.

## After calibration

Human involvement is exceptional only: the Independent Final Producer may explicitly escalate a pure taste ambiguity after all objective and scored quality gates pass. Normal productions do not require a human Creative Lock.

## Human work that is not per-job creative rescue

Separate release/certification work still requires blind human evaluation of the commercial benchmark corpus. That is system calibration/release evidence, not routine production supervision.
